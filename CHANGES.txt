GRADING CHANGE (spec):
  . , ? ! : ; quotes and brackets are ignored when comparing the typed
  answer with the expected one — missing/extra/different punctuation is
  still correct. Apostrophes inside contractions (don't, I'm) and hyphens
  stay required. Wrong words, extra words and grammar errors are still
  rejected. The displayed sentence is never modified.
